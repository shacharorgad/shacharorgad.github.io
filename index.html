    Mystery Brick

    Found a piece and don't know which set it's from? Let's find out.

    Type the part number

    Look closely at the piece, often inside or underneath. Many pieces have a tiny number molded in, like 3001 or 60581. A flashlight helps!

    No number? Take a picture

    Brickognize is a free website. Put one piece on a plain table, take a photo, and it tells you what the piece is and which sets have it.

    Open Brickognize

    Build Ideas

    Got a pile of loose bricks? Scan them with Brickit and it shows you what you can build.

    How to scan

    1. Dump your loose bricks on a table or the floor.
    2. Spread them out in one layer so no brick hides another.
    3. Open Brickit, tap Scan, and hold the iPhone about 60 cm (2 feet) above the bricks.
    4. Pick an idea and follow the steps. Brickit shows where each brick is in your pile.

    Works best with lots of bricks (150 or more). It looks at shapes, not colors, so your builds might be rainbow!

    Get Brickit on the App Store

    Brickit is free. Some extra features cost money, so ask a grown-up before buying anything.

    Backup

    Save a backup

    Your sets live only on this phone. A backup is a file with all your sets and photos. Save it to iCloud Drive or send it to Dad, so nothing gets lost if the phone breaks.

    Bring back a backup

    Got a new phone? Pick your backup file and all your sets come back.
